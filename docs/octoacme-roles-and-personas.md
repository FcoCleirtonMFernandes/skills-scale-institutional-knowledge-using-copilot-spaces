# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises. Role assignments should reflect each project's needs; one person may hold more than one role when ownership and decision authority remain clear.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers to clarify requirements and acceptance criteria.
- Coordinate with Project Managers on estimates, progress, blockers, and dependencies.
- Consult Technical Leads / Architects on technical design and engineering trade-offs.
- Collaborate with QA / Test Engineers on testability, defect investigation, and verification; quality is a shared team responsibility.

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with Product Leads on product outcomes and strategic priorities; retain responsibility for backlog prioritization.
- Work with Project Managers to align product priorities with delivery plans, dependencies, and risks.
- Collaborate with Developers, UX / Product Designers, and QA / Test Engineers to clarify and validate solutions.

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Coordinate with Product Managers on delivery plans and priority changes without taking over product backlog ownership.
- Keep Product Leads and Sponsors informed of significant trade-offs, risks, and decisions requiring escalation.
- Coordinate handoffs and dependencies across Developers, QA / Test Engineers, UX / Product Designers, Technical Leads, and Operations / Release Owners.
- Track stakeholder input and approvals, clarifying who must respond and by when.

---

## Product Leads

### Role Summary
Product Leads ensure that project decisions align with product strategy and intended customer outcomes. They partner with the Project Manager and Product Manager on priorities, scope, and escalations.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm alignment between project goals and product direction
- Help resolve priority and scope trade-offs
- Validate that success measures are meaningful
- Support decisions that affect multiple product areas

### Goals
- Keep project outcomes aligned with product strategy
- Enable timely decisions when priorities or scope change

### Typical Communication
- Product and project planning discussions
- Scope and priority decisions
- Stakeholder updates and escalations

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with Product Managers on outcomes and priorities; the Product Manager remains responsible for backlog prioritization.
- Work with Project Managers on delivery trade-offs, dependencies, and escalations.
- Provide Developers and QA / Test Engineers with product context when needed.
- Coordinate with Sponsors on strategic alignment and high-level decisions.

---

## QA / Test Engineers

### Role Summary
QA / Test Engineers help prevent defects and verify that work meets acceptance criteria and quality expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Define appropriate test strategies and coverage
- Validate acceptance criteria and document test results
- Identify and communicate quality risks
- Support regression, integration, and release-readiness testing

### Goals
- Find important issues early
- Make product quality and test status visible
- Help ensure delivered work meets user and business expectations

### Typical Communication
- Test plans, test results, and defect reports
- Collaboration during implementation and release preparation
- Quality-risk updates

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Developers to plan testability, investigate defects, and validate fixes; quality remains a shared responsibility across the team.
- Partner with Product Managers to clarify acceptance criteria.
- Share quality risks with Project Managers.
- Coordinate with Operations / Release or On-call Owners on smoke tests and post-deployment checks.

---

## UX / Product Designers

### Role Summary
UX / Product Designers research user needs and design usable experiences that support project outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Research user needs and workflows
- Create and validate user flows, prototypes, and interaction designs
- Identify usability and accessibility considerations
- Communicate design decisions and constraints to the team

### Goals
- Make product experiences useful, understandable, and accessible
- Validate design assumptions with users or other evidence

### Typical Communication
- User research findings and design reviews
- Design artifacts and handoff discussions
- Collaboration on acceptance criteria and usability validation

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers to connect user needs, product outcomes, and backlog priorities.
- Collaborate with Developers and QA / Test Engineers to clarify and validate designs.
- Keep Project Managers informed about design dependencies that may affect milestones.
- Consult relevant stakeholders when research or design decisions require their input.

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads / Architects guide technical design and help the team manage engineering quality, integration, and technical risk. They provide technical direction without replacing Developers' responsibility for implementation.

### Responsibilities
- Guide technical design and architecture decisions
- Identify technical risks, integration points, and dependencies
- Support estimation and decomposition of complex work
- Communicate technical constraints and options

### Goals
- Enable reliable, maintainable solutions
- Surface technical risks and dependencies early
- Help the team make informed engineering trade-offs

### Typical Communication
- Design discussions and technical documentation
- Code reviews and engineering planning
- Risk and dependency updates

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Support Developers with implementation guidance and design decisions; Developers remain responsible for implementing and testing their work.
- Partner with Product Managers to explain technical trade-offs that affect scope or outcomes.
- Work with Project Managers to surface dependencies, risks, and delivery impacts.
- Coordinate with QA / Test Engineers and Operations / Release or On-call Owners on testing and deployment concerns.

---

## Sponsors / Executive Sponsors

### Role Summary
Sponsors provide executive support and facilitate high-level decisions needed to keep an initiative aligned and resourced.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm strategic alignment and provide executive context
- Help resolve resource or organizational constraints
- Make or escalate decisions outside the delivery team's authority
- Review significant risks and requested decisions

### Goals
- Ensure the initiative has appropriate organizational support
- Resolve high-impact impediments
- Keep investment aligned with business priorities

### Typical Communication
- Milestone-based status updates and decision requests
- Escalations from the Project Manager or Product Lead
- Project approval or review meetings

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Receive progress, risk, and decision updates from the Project Manager.
- Partner with Product Leads on strategic alignment and high-level trade-offs.
- Rely on Product Managers for product outcomes and priority context.
- Provide high-level decisions without taking over day-to-day delivery coordination.

---

## Stakeholders

### Role Summary
Stakeholders are people or groups affected by the project or who provide relevant input, expertise, resources, or approval. Their involvement should be appropriate to their relationship to the project.

### Responsibilities
- Share relevant needs, constraints, and domain expertise
- Review proposals or deliverables when their input or approval is needed
- Raise concerns, dependencies, or impacts affecting their area
- Respond to decision requests within the agreed timeframe

### Goals
- Ensure project decisions account for relevant business, user, and operational needs
- Make necessary input and approvals available without creating avoidable delays

### Typical Communication
- Project updates and milestone reviews
- Requests for feedback, decisions, or approvals
- Workshops or reviews relevant to their area

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- The Project Manager coordinates stakeholder communication, tracks requests, and clarifies when a response is needed.
- Product Managers gather stakeholder input and use it to inform product outcomes and backlog prioritization.
- Product Leads help resolve cross-product or strategic concerns.
- Stakeholders provide input or approval within their area but do not take over day-to-day delivery coordination.

---

## Operations / Release or On-call Owners

### Role Summary
Operations / Release or On-call Owners help ensure that changes can be deployed, monitored, and supported reliably in production.

### Responsibilities
- Advise on operational readiness, deployment, monitoring, and support needs
- Coordinate or support deployment activities
- Verify production health after deployment
- Participate in incident response and communicate operational risks
- Help define rollback or mitigation steps

### Goals
- Reduce deployment and operational risk
- Make production health and support readiness visible
- Help the team respond effectively to production issues

### Typical Communication
- Release plans and readiness reviews
- Deployment checklists and monitoring dashboards
- On-call handoffs and incident communications

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Developers and Technical Leads on deployment needs, observability, and operational risks.
- Coordinate with QA / Test Engineers on smoke tests and verification plans.
- Partner with Project Managers on release timing and stakeholder communication.
- Follow the relevant incident process; for security incidents, follow the security incident runbook and notify the Security on-call. Coordinate operational response with the appropriate responders.

---

## Role Assignment Guidance

Not every project needs a separate person for each role. At project initiation, identify the responsibilities needed, the person accountable for each decision or deliverable, and a backup where appropriate. One person may hold multiple roles, but this must not make ownership, approval authority, or handoffs unclear. Validate role assignments with relevant stakeholders and revisit them when the project scope or team changes.
