## Stakeholders

### Role Summary
Stakeholders are people or groups affected by the project or who provide relevant input, expertise, resources, or approval. Their involvement should be appropriate to their relationship to the project.

### Responsibilities
- Share relevant needs, constraints, and domain expertise
- Review proposals or deliverables when their input or approval is needed
- Raise concerns, dependencies, or impacts affecting their area
- Respond to decision requests within the agreed timeframe

### Goals
- Ensure project decisions account for relevant business, user, and operational needs
- Make necessary input and approvals available without creating avoidable delays

### Typical Communication
- Project updates and milestone reviews
- Requests for feedback, decisions, or approvals
- Workshops or reviews relevant to their area

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- The Project Manager coordinates stakeholder communication, tracks requests, and clarifies when a response is needed.
- Product Managers gather stakeholder input and use it to inform product outcomes and backlog prioritization.
- Product Leads help resolve cross-product or strategic concerns.
- Stakeholders provide input or approval within their area but do not take over day-to-day delivery coordination.
