## Product Leads

### Role Summary
Product Leads ensure that project decisions align with product strategy and intended customer outcomes. They partner with the Project Manager and Product Manager on priorities, scope, and escalations.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm alignment between project goals and product direction
- Help resolve priority and scope trade-offs
- Validate that success measures are meaningful
- Support decisions that affect multiple product areas

### Goals
- Keep project outcomes aligned with product strategy
- Enable timely decisions when priorities or scope change

### Typical Communication
- Product and project planning discussions
- Scope and priority decisions
- Stakeholder updates and escalations

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers on outcomes and priorities; the Product Manager remains responsible for backlog prioritization.
- Works with Project Managers on delivery trade-offs, dependencies, and escalations.
- Provides Developers and QA / Test Engineers with product context when needed.

## QA / Test Engineers

### Role Summary
QA / Test Engineers help prevent defects and verify that work meets acceptance criteria and quality expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Define appropriate test strategies and coverage
- Validate acceptance criteria and document test results
- Identify and communicate quality risks
- Support regression, integration, and release-readiness testing

### Goals
- Find important issues early
- Make product quality and test status visible
- Help ensure delivered work meets user and business expectations

### Typical Communication
- Test plans, test results, and defect reports
- Collaboration during implementation and release preparation
- Quality-risk updates

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to plan testability, investigate defects, and validate fixes.
- Partners with Product Managers to clarify acceptance criteria.
- Shares quality risks with Project Managers.
- Coordinates with Operations / Release Owners on smoke tests and post-deployment checks.

## UX / Product Designers

### Role Summary
UX / Product Designers research user needs and design usable experiences that support project outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Research user needs and workflows
- Create and validate user flows, prototypes, and interaction designs
- Identify usability and accessibility considerations
- Communicate design decisions and constraints to the team

### Goals
- Make product experiences useful, understandable, and accessible
- Validate design assumptions with users or other evidence

### Typical Communication
- User research findings and design reviews
- Design artifacts and handoff discussions
- Collaboration on acceptance criteria and usability validation

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to connect user needs, product outcomes, and backlog priorities.
- Collaborates with Developers and QA / Test Engineers to clarify and validate designs.
- Keeps Project Managers informed about design dependencies that may affect milestones.

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads / Architects guide technical design and help the team manage engineering quality, integration, and technical risk.

### Responsibilities
- Guide technical design and architecture decisions
- Identify technical risks, integration points, and dependencies
- Support estimation and decomposition of complex work
- Communicate technical constraints and options

### Goals
- Enable reliable, maintainable solutions
- Surface technical risks and dependencies early
- Help the team make informed engineering trade-offs

### Typical Communication
- Design discussions and technical documentation
- Code reviews and engineering planning
- Risk and dependency updates

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Supports Developers with implementation guidance and design decisions.
- Partners with Product Managers to explain technical trade-offs that affect scope or outcomes.
- Works with Project Managers to surface dependencies, risks, and delivery impacts.
- Coordinates with QA / Test Engineers and Operations / Release Owners on testing and deployment concerns.

## Sponsors / Executive Sponsors

### Role Summary
Sponsors provide executive support and facilitate high-level decisions needed to keep an initiative aligned and resourced.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm strategic alignment and provide executive context
- Help resolve resource or organizational constraints
- Make or escalate decisions outside the delivery team’s authority
- Review significant risks and requested decisions

### Goals
- Ensure the initiative has appropriate organizational support
- Resolve high-impact impediments
- Keep investment aligned with business priorities

### Typical Communication
- Milestone-based status updates and decision requests
- Escalations from the Project Manager or Product Lead
- Project approval or review meetings

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Receives progress, risk, and decision updates from the Project Manager.
- Partners with Product Leads on strategic alignment and high-level trade-offs.
- Relies on Product Managers for product outcomes and priority context.
- Provides high-level decisions without taking over day-to-day delivery coordination.

## Operations / Release or On-call Owners

### Role Summary
Operations / Release or On-call Owners help ensure that changes can be deployed, monitored, and supported reliably in production.

### Responsibilities
- Advise on operational readiness, deployment, monitoring, and support needs
- Coordinate or support deployment activities
- Verify production health after deployment
- Participate in incident response and communicate operational risks
- Help define rollback or mitigation steps

### Goals
- Reduce deployment and operational risk
- Make production health and support readiness visible
- Help the team respond effectively to production issues

### Typical Communication
- Release plans and readiness reviews
- Deployment checklists and monitoring dashboards
- On-call handoffs and incident communications

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers and Technical Leads on deployment needs, observability, and operational risks.
- Coordinates with QA / Test Engineers on smoke tests and verification plans.
- Partners with Project Managers on release timing and stakeholder communication.
- Follows the relevant incident and security escalation processes.

---

## Role Assignment Guidance

Not every project needs a separate person for each role. At project initiation, identify which responsibilities are needed, who owns them, and who provides backup. One person may hold multiple roles when appropriate, but decision ownership and handoffs should remain clear.
